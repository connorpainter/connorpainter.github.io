High-redshift Star Clusters in CampFIRE

For the last year and a half, I have been analyzing the BonFIRE + CampFIRE suite of cosmological simulations for star clusters at high redshift. The simulations form a representative sample of galaxies across five orders of magnitude in stellar mass, many of which feature highly clustered star formation. In an upcoming publication, I plan to detail the simulation's predictions on star cluster properties at redshift 9 and above, and how they contribute to their host galaxies. Additionally, I will present a new algorithm for identifying star clusters in a particle-based simulation. I hope to link a GitHub repository to the algorithm soon!
