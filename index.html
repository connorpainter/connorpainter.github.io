High-redshift Star Clusters in CampFIRE

I analyze the BonFIRE + CampFIRE suite of cosmological simulations for star clusters at high redshift in a representative sample of galaxies with a wide range of stellar masses. I present a new cluster-finding algorithm applicable to any particle-based simulation.

An attractive model: simulating fuzzy dark matter with attractive self-interactions

I simulated dark matter halos, assuming the dark matter particle was a self-interacting axion. I provided a new fitting function to the density profile of the resulting halos.
